Results

Scaling dense 3D tracking to full-length video.

TrackEverything is the first dense 3D point tracker that tracks all points across all frames in videos of 1000+ frames — outperforming open-source dense 3D trackers by over 20% APD on TAPVid-3D while running in under 40 GB of GPU memory.

Method

What is TrackEverything?

Videos are 2D projections of a persistent 3D world, yet most tracking methods operate in pixel space, redundantly re-representing the same surfaces across frames. We introduce TrackEverything, a dense 3D point tracker that represents videos as persistent 3D scene tracks in world coordinates.

Our approach combines three ideas: (1) tracking in 3D scene-content space, where repeated observations of the same surface are fused and occluded points persist without redundant representation; (2) a recurrent architecture that iteratively refines motion estimates and chains predictions across sliding windows for long-video inference; and (3) per-window static/dynamic classification that allocates trajectory decoding only to points in motion. At each window boundary, the 3D representation is voxelized to remove redundant points, ensuring model complexity scales with distinct scene content rather than video length.
